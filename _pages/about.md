Hi, I'm Pietro Sillano, a **PhD researcher** in biophysics at the [Kavli Institute of Nanoscience](https://kavli.tudelft.nl/), **Delft University of Technology**, and at the **University of Groningen** (Groningen Biomolecular Sciences and Biotechnology Institute).

My research sits at the intersection of **soft matter physics** and **computational biology**. I develop mesoscale models for lipid bilayer membranes — bridging the gap between atomistic simulations and continuum mechanics to access the length and time scales relevant for cellular processes. My current project is [MesoMem](/mesomem/), a coarse-grained membrane model implemented in LAMMPS.

More broadly, I'm interested in **statistical physics**, **molecular dynamics**, and how computational frameworks can shed light on complex biological phenomena.

I'm also a member of the <a href="https://www.mljc.it/"><b>Machine Learning Journal Club</b></a>, a non-profit research organization from Turin focused on interdisciplinary applications of ML and deep learning.
